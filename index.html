Our Story

Started around a kitchen table, now a growing circle

Uthaan began with five women meeting weekly to talk — about grief, restarting careers, and rebuilding confidence after difficult years. What started as conversation became a structured space for emotional support and skill-building.

Today we run counseling circles, mentorship programs, and training sessions — but the heart of it is still the same: show up, listen, and walk forward together.

— The Uthaan Team
